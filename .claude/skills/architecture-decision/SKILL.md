# Architecture Decision Record

Create an ADR with:

- Title
- Status
- Context
- Decision drivers
- Options considered
- Decision
- Consequences
- Risks
- Migration/rollback notes

Rules:
- Compare realistic alternatives.
- Record why an option was chosen, not just what was chosen.
- Avoid permanent complexity for hypothetical future scale.
- Identify reversibility and switching cost.
